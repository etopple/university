---
title: "✏️ How to Edit and Publish a University Page"
description: >-
  A step-by-step guide for eTop staff: change a page on eTop University, check
  it, publish it, and undo a mistake.
---
Anyone at eTop can fix a typo, update a step, or add a note to a page on eTop University. You do it in your web browser, with nothing to install.

You can't break the live site by saving. Visitors only see your change after you click **Publish changes**.

## Before you start

- You need your eTop Microsoft account, the same one you use for Outlook and Teams.
- The first time you sign in, the editor creates your account. If it says you don't have permission to do something, ask BJ (William Pote).

## 1. Open the editor

Go to **https://university.etop.tech/_emdash/admin** and sign in with your eTop email address. If your phone asks you to approve the sign-in, approve it.

![The Microsoft sign-in screen you see when you open the editor](/images/editing-university/01-sign-in.png)

The very first time, a **Welcome** box appears. Click **Get Started** to close it.

:::tip
Already looking at the page you want to change? Scroll to the bottom of it and click **Edit page**. You'll sign in first (step 1), then land straight on step 3.
:::

## 2. Find the page

1. Click **Docs** in the menu on the left.
2. Type a word from the page's title into **Search docs...**
3. Click the page's title.

![The Docs list, with the search box and the page title highlighted](/images/editing-university/02-find-the-page.png)

## 3. Make your change

The editor shows the page in two main boxes:

- **Title** is the big heading at the top of the page.
- **Description** is the short summary that shows up in search results. You can usually leave it alone.
- **Body** is everything else on the page. Click where you want to type, just like in Word. The buttons above it make text bold, add lists and headings, and add links.

![The editor, with the Title and Body boxes highlighted](/images/editing-university/03-the-editor.png)

Here, one sentence was added to the end of the first paragraph:

![The first paragraph in the Body box, with a new sentence at the end](/images/editing-university/04-make-your-change.png)

## 4. Save

Click **Save** at the top right.

![The Save button at the top right of the editor](/images/editing-university/05-save.png)

Saving keeps your change as a **draft**. The panel on the right now says **Draft changes**, and visitors still see the old version.

![After saving: the panel shows "Draft changes" and a "Publish changes" button](/images/editing-university/06-after-save.png)

- To see how the page will look, click **Preview draft**.
- To throw your change away, click **Discard changes**.

## 5. Publish

1. Click **Publish changes**.

   ![The Publish changes button](/images/editing-university/07-publish.png)

2. A box asks you to confirm. Click **Publish changes** again.

   ![The confirmation box, with the Publish changes button highlighted](/images/editing-university/08-confirm.png)

The panel now says **Live version**, and visitors see your change.

## 6. Check the live page

Click **Live View** at the top right to open the page on the site and check your change.

![The page on the University site, with the new sentence at the end of the first paragraph](/images/editing-university/11-live.png)

## Undo a mistake

Every time you save, the editor keeps a copy. To go back to an earlier version:

1. In the editor, scroll down the right-hand column and click **Revisions**.
2. Each version shows the date and time it was saved. Find the one from just before your change and click the round arrow next to it. Not sure which one? Ask BJ before you restore.

   ![The Revisions list, with the round "restore" arrow highlighted](/images/editing-university/10-revisions.png)

3. Click **Restore**.

   ![The box that asks you to confirm the restore](/images/editing-university/12-restore-confirm.png)

4. The old version comes back as a draft. Click **Preview draft** to check it, then **Publish changes** to put it on the live site.

## Leave these alone

These change more than the page's words. Ask BJ before using them:

- **Unpublish Doc** (just under Save) takes the whole page off the website.
- **Slug** (under *URL & language*) is the page's web address. Changing it breaks every link to the page.
- **Move to Trash** and the red bin icons in the Docs list delete the page.
- **Pictures:** adding a new picture isn't switched on yet. If a page needs one, ask BJ.

## Good to know

- **A brand-new page** also needs a spot in the menu on the left. Ask BJ to add one for you.
- **Stuck, or not sure?** Save your change without publishing and ask BJ to look. Nobody sees a draft until it's published.
