---
title: "CloudFlare Zero Trust for iOS"
description: Protecting your mobile device connections
---
Here's how to set up your iPhone or iPad to connect to Cloudflare Zero trust network for your office.

1. Install the certificate. Contact eTop to get the Cloudflare Certificate installed on your iPad or iPhone\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (117).png>)\
   Click Close\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (118).png>)
2. Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > Gateway CA - Cloudflare Managed ....\

3. Tap Install on the top right\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (120).png>)
4. Type in the passcode for your device, then Done\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (121).png>)
5. Go back to General > About > then Certificate Trust settings\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (122).png>)
6. Enable the Gateway CA - Cloudflare Managed ....... certificate, then click Continue\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (123).png>)
7. The certificate is now installed. Now we go to the Cloudflare One app\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (124).png>)
8. Back in the Cloudflare One app, tap Next\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (131).png>)
9. Accept terms and conditions\
   ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (132).png>)
10. Enter your Team Name, then Next at the top right\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (133).png>)
11. Tap Continue:\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (134).png>)
12. Tap to sign in with AzureAD - AzureAD. If needed this is your work email address and password. Note you may have to use Multifactor Authentication.\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (135).png>)\

13. Install VPN Profile:\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (125).png>)
14. Tap Allow:\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (126).png>)
15. Enter Passcode:\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (127).png>)
16. You should then be able to use Zero Trust to secure your iOS device's connection and connect to company resources\
    ![](</.gitbook/assets/image (136).png>)
