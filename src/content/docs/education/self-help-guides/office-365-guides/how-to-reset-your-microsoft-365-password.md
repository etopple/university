# 🔑 How to Reset Your Microsoft 365 Password

If you've forgotten your password or your account is locked out, you can often reset it yourself without contacting the helpdesk. Microsoft's Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR) lets you get back into your account in just a few minutes.

---

## Option 1: Reset from the Sign-In Screen

1. Go to [https://login.microsoftonline.com](https://login.microsoftonline.com) and enter your email address.
2. On the password screen, click **"Forgot my password"**.
3. Complete the CAPTCHA verification to prove you're not a robot.
4. Choose a verification method:
   * **Text my mobile phone** — You'll receive a code via SMS.
   * **Email my alternate email** — A code will be sent to your recovery email.
   * **Microsoft Authenticator app** — Approve the notification on your phone.
5. Enter the verification code when prompted.
6. Create a new password that meets the following requirements:
   * At least **14 characters** long
   * Contains a mix of **uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols**
   * Is **not** a password you've used before
7. Click **Submit** — you're back in!

---

## Option 2: Change Your Password (When You Know Your Current One)

If you can still sign in but want to change your password:

1. Go to [https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info).
2. Sign in with your current credentials.
3. Click **"Change password"**.
4. Enter your old password and your new password twice.
5. Click **Submit**.

---

## My Reset Link Expired — What Do I Do?

Password reset links are only valid for a short period of time. If your link has expired:

* Go back to the sign-in page and click **"Forgot my password"** again to generate a fresh link.
* Complete the process promptly — don't leave the page open and come back later.

---

## Tips for Creating a Strong Password

* Use a **passphrase** — a string of random words is easy to remember and hard to crack (e.g., `BlueTruck$Running42!`).
* **Never reuse passwords** across different services.
* Consider using your company's **password manager** to store and generate strong passwords.
* **Never share your password** with anyone, including IT staff.
