# 🖨️ Troubleshooting Common Printer Issues

Printer problems are one of the most common IT issues in any office. Before submitting a ticket, try these troubleshooting steps — many printer issues can be resolved in just a few minutes.

---

## My Printer Won't Print

### Step 1: Check the Basics

* Is the printer **powered on** and showing a ready/green status light?
* Is there **paper** in the tray? Is the paper tray fully seated?
* Are there any **error lights or messages** on the printer's display panel?
* Is the printer connected to the **network** (check for a network/Wi-Fi icon on the display)?

### Step 2: Verify the Correct Printer is Selected

* When you print, make sure you're sending the job to the **correct printer** — not "Microsoft Print to PDF" or another device.
* In your application, go to **File → Print** and check the printer name in the dropdown.

### Step 3: Restart the Print Spooler

If your computer shows the print job as sent but nothing happens:

1. Press **Windows Key + R**, type `services.msc`, and press Enter.
2. Scroll down to **Print Spooler**.
3. Right-click it and select **Restart**.
4. Try printing again.

> **Quick Alternative:** Restart your computer — this also restarts the Print Spooler.

---

## I Have Stuck or Failed Print Jobs in the Queue

1. Click the **printer icon** in your system tray (bottom-right of your screen near the clock).
2. If you see pending print jobs, select them all (**Ctrl + A**) and press **Delete**.
3. Try printing a fresh test page.

If jobs keep getting stuck after clearing the queue, restart your computer and try again.

---

## Setting a Default Printer

Tired of selecting the right printer every time?

1. Open **Settings** → **Bluetooth & devices** → **Printers & scanners**.
2. Turn **off** "Let Windows manage my default printer" at the bottom of the page.
3. Click on the printer you want as your default.
4. Click **"Set as default"**.

---

## Printing Double-Sided (Duplex)

1. Open your document and go to **File → Print**.
2. Look for a setting called **"Print on Both Sides"**, **"Duplex Printing"**, or **"Two-Sided"**.
3. Choose either:
   * **Flip on Long Edge** — for standard documents (like a book).
   * **Flip on Short Edge** — for landscape or top-bound documents.
4. Click **Print**.

> **Note:** Not all printers support automatic duplex printing. If the option doesn't appear, your printer may require manual flipping.

---

## Printing from a Mobile Device

### iPhone / iPad (AirPrint)
* Make sure your phone and printer are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
* Open the document or photo, tap the **Share** icon, and select **Print**.
* Choose your printer from the list.

### Android
* Open the document, tap the **three-dot menu** → **Print**.
* Select your printer. If it doesn't appear, you may need to install the printer manufacturer's app from the Play Store.

> **Note:** Not all office printers support mobile printing. If you're having trouble, try emailing the document to yourself and printing from your computer.

---

## Printer Not Showing Up at All

If your printer doesn't appear in the printer list:

1. Make sure the printer is **powered on and connected to the network**.
2. Restart your computer.
3. Go to **Settings** → **Bluetooth & devices** → **Printers & scanners** → **Add device** and see if it appears.
4. If it still doesn't show up, the printer may need its **driver reinstalled** — contact eTop for help.
