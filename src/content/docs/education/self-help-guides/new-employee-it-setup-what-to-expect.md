# 🆕 New Employee IT Setup: What to Expect

Welcome aboard! This guide explains what happens with your technology setup as a new employee, what you'll receive, and how to get up and running quickly.

---

## Before Your First Day

Your manager will work with eTop Technology to get your accounts and equipment ready. Here's what's typically set up before you arrive:

* **Microsoft 365 account** — Your email, Teams, OneDrive, and Office apps
* **Computer/laptop** — Configured with the applications you'll need
* **Security tools** — Antivirus, endpoint protection, and monitoring software
* **Printer access** — Configured for your office location
* **VPN access** (if applicable) — For remote access to office resources

> **Note:** Some items may take a few days depending on equipment availability and account provisioning. Your manager will communicate the timeline.

---

## On Your First Day

### 1. Sign In to Your Computer

You'll receive your initial login credentials (usually from your manager or IT). Your first sign-in will typically prompt you to:

* **Set a new password** — Choose something strong (at least 14 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols).
* **Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** — You'll need your personal mobile phone for this step. See our [MFA Setup Guide](office-365-guides/microsoft-authenticator/setting-up-mfa-on-a-new-phone.md).

### 2. Set Up Microsoft Outlook

Outlook is your email and calendar. It should be pre-configured, but if prompted:

1. Open Outlook from the Start menu or taskbar.
2. Enter your email address when prompted.
3. Sign in with your new password.
4. Approve the MFA prompt on your phone.

### 3. Sign In to Microsoft Teams

Teams is how your team communicates and collaborates:

1. Open Teams from the taskbar or Start menu.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Your manager should add you to the appropriate Teams channels.

For a guide on using Teams, see: [Using Teams](../etop-tools/office-365-guides/using-teams.md)

### 4. Verify Printer Access

Try printing a test page to make sure your printer is connected:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Bluetooth & devices** → **Printers & scanners**.
2. Find your office printer in the list.
3. Click on it and select **"Print test page"**.

If you don't see a printer, see our [Printer Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-common-printer-issues.md).

## Setting Up Your Mobile Phone (Optional)

If your organization uses mobile device management:

1. Download **Microsoft Outlook** and **Microsoft Teams** from your phone's app store.
2. Sign in with your work email and password.
3. You may be prompted to install **Microsoft Company Portal** — this helps your organization secure work data on your phone.

For detailed mobile setup guides, see:
* [iOS Phone Setup](office-365-guides/mobile-phone-setup/ios-phone-setup.md)
* [Android Phone Setup](office-365-guides/mobile-phone-setup/android-phone-setup.md)

## What You'll Need to Provide

To make your setup as smooth as possible, have the following ready:

* Your **personal mobile phone** — for MFA setup
* Any **software or application requests** specific to your role — let your manager know so they can be included in your setup
* Your **preferred display name** (if different from your legal name) — let your manager know
