## 🖱️ Three Quick Fixes for Common Computer Issues

If your computer isn’t behaving the way it should, don’t panic — most issues can be resolved in just a few quick steps. Before reaching out for help, try these simple fixes that often solve the problem right away.

***

### 1️⃣ Restart Your Computer

Yes, it really does work! Restarting clears out temporary processes, refreshes memory, and resets services that may have gotten stuck.

> **Tip:** Always restart — don’t just shut down — to ensure all updates and processes reload correctly.

***

### 2️⃣ Check Your Connections

Loose cables or peripherals can cause everything from display glitches to keyboard or mouse issues.

* Unplug and reconnect your monitor, power cable, and accessories.
* If you’re on a laptop dock, reseat it or unplug and reconnect.

> **Tip:** You’d be surprised how often this step resolves odd computer behavior.

***

### 3️⃣ Clear Your Browser Cache & Cookies

If your issue is web-related — like slow pages, login loops, or broken buttons — your browser may be holding onto outdated data.

* In Chrome or Edge, press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete** (Windows) or **Command + Shift + Delete** (Mac).
* Choose **Cached images and files** and **Cookies**, then clear data.

> **Tip:** After clearing, sign back in to your sites to start fresh.
