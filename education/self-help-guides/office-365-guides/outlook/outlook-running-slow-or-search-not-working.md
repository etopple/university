# 🐌 Outlook Running Slow or Search Not Working

If Outlook is taking forever to open, emails are slow to load, or search isn't returning results, these steps will help you get things running smoothly again.

---

## Outlook Is Slow to Open or Respond

### Step 1: Restart Outlook (and Your Computer)

It sounds simple, but fully closing and reopening Outlook resolves most temporary slowness:

1. **Close Outlook** completely (don't just minimize it).
2. If Outlook is frozen, press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete** → **Task Manager** → find **Microsoft Outlook** → click **End task**.
3. Reopen Outlook.
4. If it's still slow, **restart your computer** — this clears cached processes.

### Step 2: Disable Unnecessary Add-ins

Add-ins can significantly slow down Outlook:

1. In Outlook, go to **File** → **Options** → **Add-ins**.
2. At the bottom, set the dropdown to **"COM Add-ins"** and click **Go**.
3. Uncheck any add-ins you don't recognize or don't need.
4. Click **OK** and restart Outlook.

> **Tip:** If you're not sure what an add-in does, uncheck it. If something stops working, you can re-enable it later.

### Step 3: Repair Your Outlook Profile

A corrupted profile is a common cause of persistent slowness:

1. Close Outlook.
2. Go to **Control Panel** → **Mail** → **Show Profiles**.
3. Select your profile and click **Properties** → **Email Accounts** → **Repair**.
4. Follow the prompts and restart Outlook.

## Search Is Not Returning Results

### Step 1: Rebuild the Search Index

1. In Outlook, go to **File** → **Options** → **Search** → **Indexing Options**.
2. Click **Advanced** → **Rebuild**.
3. Click **OK** and wait — rebuilding can take 15-30 minutes depending on your mailbox size.
4. Try searching again after the rebuild completes.

### Step 2: Check Search Scope

Make sure you're searching in the right place:

* **Current Folder** vs **All Mailboxes** — Click the search bar and check the scope dropdown. If you're only searching "Current Folder," you may be missing results.
* Try typing your search and then clicking **"All Outlook Items"** to expand the scope.

### Step 3: Use the New Outlook Search

If you're using the **New Outlook** (the toggle at the top-right), search works differently:

* The search bar is at the top of the window.
* Results include emails, contacts, files, and calendar events.
* Use **filters** (From, Has Attachment, Date) to narrow results.

---

## Emails Are Not Arriving or Notifications Are Delayed

1. Check your **internet connection** — open a browser and make sure you can reach websites.
2. Click **Send/Receive** → **Send/Receive All Folders** (or press **F9**).
3. Check your **Focused Inbox** — emails may be landing in the **"Other"** tab instead of **"Focused"**.
4. Check your **Junk/Spam** folder — legitimate emails sometimes get filtered.
5. Check your **Quarantine** — see our guide on [Managing Quarantine](managing-quarantine.md) for details.

---

## Outlook Uses Too Much Memory

If Outlook is using a lot of RAM and slowing down your computer:

* **Reduce your mailbox size:** Archive old emails or delete items you no longer need. See our guide on [Online Archiving](online-archiving.md).
* **Close extra Outlook windows:** Each open calendar, contact, or email window uses additional memory.
* **Disable the Reading Pane** temporarily: **View** → **Reading Pane** → **Off**.
